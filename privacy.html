Information Stacks stores

When you create an account, the authentication provider stores your email address and account credentials. Stacks stores book titles, authors, page counts, cover paths, publication choices, and uploaded PDF files in the configured Supabase project. PDFs are private to the account that uploaded them. If you choose “Publish the book listing,” the title, author, page count, and cover become visible to visitors. The PDF itself remains private.

Recommendation preferences, saved titles, reading position, and feedback are currently saved in your browser. Stacks does not send those preferences to its database.

Service providers

Stacks uses Supabase for account sign-in, database records, and file storage. The app also contacts Open Library, Project Gutenberg, and the configured hosting provider when you use those features. Review their privacy terms before launch and add any other provider used by your deployment.
